Privacy Policy

NetLimiter • Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NetLimiter ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our Android application. NetLimiter monitors per-app network data usage on your device and helps you limit, warn about, and automatically block apps that exceed the data quotas you set.

Privacy Commitment: NetLimiter is designed to work on your device. Your app usage statistics, data quotas, firewall rules, notes, and audit logs are stored only in the App's private, encrypted storage. We do not run an account system, and we do not upload, sell, or share your app usage or network information with our own servers.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information on our servers. The App has no account system, does not require registration, and does not transmit your usage data to us. All functionality is performed locally on your device.

2. Information Processed and Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide its data-limiting features, NetLimiter reads and stores the following information only on your device:

  • Installed applications: Package names, app labels, and Android UIDs of apps installed on your device (used to build the list of apps you can monitor and limit).
  • Network data usage: Per-app data consumed over Wi-Fi and cellular networks, split into foreground and background usage, obtained from Android's NetworkStatsManager and TrafficStats system APIs.
  • Quota rules and limits: The data limits, reset periods (hourly/daily), warning thresholds, priority and whitelist status, and block settings you configure for each app.
  • Usage logs and audit events: Records of when limits were configured, warnings were issued at 80%/90%, limits were breached, and apps were automatically or manually blocked.
  • Notes: Any optional text notes you attach to an app's rule (stored encrypted on your device).
  • Preferences: Settings such as theme mode, custom reset time, global data saver state and cap, and your whitelist of essential system services.
  • Device status signals: Battery level, charging state, and local time, used only for optional automatic theme switching.

3. Local VPN / Firewall Protection

NetLimiter uses Android's VpnService to enforce the limits you set. When an app reaches its quota, NetLimiter establishes a local, on-device tunnel for that app and discards (blackholes) its network packets, which blocks its internet access until the limit resets or you unblock it.

Important: The VPN used by NetLimiter is a local filtering interface only. It does not route your traffic through our servers, and we do not inspect, read, decrypt, log, or store the content of your network traffic, browsing history, or messages. No traffic data is sent to us or to any third party by this feature.

4. How We Use Information

All locally stored information is used solely to provide the App's features:

  • Measuring and displaying per-app data consumption and usage trends.
  • Enforcing the quota limits, daily caps, and global data saver rules you configure.
  • Sending warning notifications when an app reaches 80%, 90%, or 100% of its limit.
  • Blocking and unblocking an app's network access through the local VPN firewall.
  • Maintaining an on-device audit log of events for your own review.
  • Generating CSV/PDF usage reports that you explicitly choose to export or share.
  • Applying your display, theme, reset-time, and whitelist preferences.

5. Storage, Encryption, and Retention

Your rules, notes, usage statistics, and audit logs are stored in the App's private database on your device and are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before being committed. This data stays on your device. There is no cloud sync or backup of this data to our systems.

You can clear individual audit logs, reset all usage statistics, or disable monitoring at any time from within the App. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data permanently removes all locally stored information.

6. App Permissions

NetLimiter requests only the permissions required for its core functionality:

  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Required to read per-app network usage statistics from Android's system APIs. You grant this manually in system Settings.
  • Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): Required to list the apps installed on your device so you can choose which ones to monitor and limit.
  • VPN Service (BIND_VPN_SERVICE): Required for the on-device firewall that blocks apps when they exceed their limits. You must approve the VPN connection prompt.
  • Internet & Network State: Used by the advertising service to load ads and check connectivity.
  • Foreground Service: Keeps the traffic monitor and firewall running reliably.
  • Post Notifications: Sends quota warnings, limit-breach alerts, and the ongoing monitoring status.
  • Receive Boot Completed: Restores monitoring and firewall protection after your device restarts.
  • Vibrate: Used for notification feedback.

NetLimiter does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, photos, or personal files.

7. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The App includes Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner, interstitial, and native ads). AdMob may independently collect certain information to serve ads, such as device identifiers (including the advertising ID), device information, IP address (which may be used to infer approximate location), and ad interaction data. This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's Google Ads settings.

The App may also use Google Firebase / Google Play services components (such as App Check) to verify app integrity and protect against abuse. These services may process device and app-integrity signals but do not receive your app usage statistics, quota rules, notes, or traffic content. The App does not use analytics or crash-reporting services that receive your usage data.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your app usage statistics, quota rules, notes, audit logs, or traffic data with anyone. The only data shared with third parties comes from Google AdMob's ad-serving mechanisms described above. AdMob does not receive access to your usage statistics or traffic content.

When you choose to export a CSV or PDF report, the file is created on your device and shared only through Android's share sheet to the destination you select. We never receive a copy.

9. Data Security

Keeping your usage data on your device and encrypting it at rest helps limit unnecessary exposure, but no electronic storage method is completely secure. Please use your device's screen lock and review the permissions you grant to the App. Because the App can block network access for other apps, you are responsible for configuring limits appropriately and for any consequences of applying them.

10. Responsible Use

NetLimiter is intended to be installed and used by the owner of the device, or by an administrator with the knowledge and consent of the device owner or user. You should not use the App to monitor or restrict another person's device without their consent and without complying with applicable laws. You are solely responsible for how you use the App on devices you do not personally own.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the App, please contact us.
