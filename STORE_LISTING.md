## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Take control of your mobile data with NetLimiter — the app-level data limiter and firewall for Android.

NetLimiter tracks how much data each app uses over Wi-Fi and mobile networks, lets you set daily or hourly limits, and automatically blocks apps that exceed your quota. No account, no cloud, no tracking — everything stays on your device.

KEY FEATURES
• Per-app data tracking – see Wi-Fi, mobile, foreground and background usage for every app
• Custom quota limits – set hourly or daily data caps for any app
• Automatic blocking – cut off an app's internet access the moment it hits its limit
• Global Data Saver – enforce a strict daily cap across all non-essential apps
• Early warnings – get notified at 80% and 90% before you run out
• Priority & whitelist – keep messaging, calls and essential services always online
• Local VPN firewall – blocking happens on your device; your traffic is never routed through our servers
• Usage history & trends – review daily, weekly, monthly and yearly data patterns
• Export reports – generate CSV or PDF usage reports to share
• Home-screen widgets – glanceable usage and one-tap block controls
• Dark / light / system themes with automatic switching

PRIVACY FIRST
NetLimiter is designed to work entirely on your device. Your app usage, limits and notes are stored locally and encrypted. We don't require an account and we don't upload or sell your data.

Note: NetLimiter requires Usage Access to read per-app data statistics and a local VPN connection to enforce limits. The VPN is a local filter only and does not send your traffic anywhere.

Take charge of your data usage today with NetLimiter.
```
